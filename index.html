Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2, 2025

GLLORY is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect when you use the GLLORY mobile application, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only what is necessary to provide the app's features:

  • Account information — email address and password (stored securely via Supabase Auth).
  • Profile information — name, age, and skin type that you enter during onboarding.
  • Scan data — face and dental scan results generated entirely on your device. Raw camera images are processed locally and never uploaded to our servers.
  • Product scan data — photos of product labels you choose to scan are sent to Google's Gemini API for ingredient analysis and then discarded. We do not store these images.
  • Usage data — anonymous crash reports and error logs (via Sentry) to help us fix bugs. No personally identifiable information is included in these reports.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To generate personalised skin and dental health scores on your device.
  • To analyse product ingredients and provide compatibility scores.
  • To send optional daily scan reminder notifications (only if you grant permission).
  • To improve app stability using anonymised crash data.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with advertisers or third-party marketers.

3. Data Storage

Scan results, your routine, and preferences are stored locally on your device using encrypted AsyncStorage. Your account credentials are stored securely by Supabase in accordance with their privacy policy.

Product label images sent for AI analysis are transmitted to Google Gemini API over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Google's data handling is governed by their privacy policy.

4. Data Retention

Local data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. Account data held by Supabase is deleted within 30 days of an account deletion request. You can request deletion by contacting us at the email below.

5. Children's Privacy

GLLORY is not directed at children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us immediately.

6. Not a Medical Device

GLLORY is not a medical device. Skin scores, health insights, and product compatibility ratings are for informational and educational purposes only. They are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for medical concerns.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and associated data.
  • Object to or restrict certain processing of your data.
  • Data portability (receive a copy of your data in a machine-readable format).

To exercise any of these rights, email us at yelmastafi@gmail.com.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including HTTPS encryption for all data in transit and Supabase's built-in security for account data. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure; however, we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your information.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
